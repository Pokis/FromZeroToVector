C# Engineering Tip (Two-Stage Binary Rescoring):
Keep 1-bit Binary Quantized vectors in RAM (`ulong[]`) and scan 10M vectors in milliseconds using hardware BitOperations.PopCount(a ^ b). Fetch the top 50 candidates from disk as full float32 to rescore with TensorPrimitives.CosineSimilarity—getting 32x RAM savings with ~99% recall!
